# DANAVA WORK — Product gap audit

## Current pages

- Login and registration
- Workspace selection and creation
- Workspace boards
- Workspace premium dashboard
- Board/Kanban
- Task detail

## Recommended missing pages

### P0 — needed for a complete team workflow

1. **Sprint & Backlog** — create, plan, start, close and review sprints; move tasks between backlog and sprint.
2. **Workspace members & roles** — invite members, assign Admin/Manager/Member/Viewer roles, deactivate access.
3. **My work** — tasks assigned to the signed-in user across all boards, grouped by today, upcoming and overdue.
4. **Notifications** — assignments, mentions, deadline warnings, comments and status changes.

### P1 — management and reporting

5. **Reports** — date filters, member productivity, overdue trends, cycle time and downloadable reports.
6. **Workspace activity** — searchable audit timeline across boards, tasks and members.
7. **Calendar** — monthly/weekly view for start dates, deadlines and sprint windows.
8. **Workspace settings** — profile, branding, board defaults, permissions and archive/trash.

### P2 — maturity and scale

9. **Templates** — reusable workspace, board, sprint and checklist templates.
10. **Global search** — search workspaces, boards, tasks, comments and files.
11. **Automation rules** — status, assignment and deadline triggers.
12. **Integrations** — email, Zalo/Telegram, storage and webhooks.

## Recommended delivery order

Sprint & Backlog → Members & Roles → My Work → Notifications → Reports → Activity → Calendar → Settings → Templates → Automation.
